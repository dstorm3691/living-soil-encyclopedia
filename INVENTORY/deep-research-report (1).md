## D. NC State Extension blanket reuse

I found **no blanket open-image reuse statement** covering photographs merely because they appear in `content.ces.ncsu.edu` publications. The opposite pattern appears in NC State's current guidance: its image-use guidance treats images as copyrighted and puts the burden on the user to know that they have legal rights to an image, while Extension marketing materials describe NC State imagery as copyright-protected. citeturn16search0turn16search3

NC State publication pages can, however, carry **image-specific** open licensing. Search results include examples where an individual image is expressly credited with a Creative Commons licence. citeturn16search2 That makes the safest compliance rule: **do not infer an open licence from the `content.ces.ncsu.edu` domain; clear each photograph from the licence/credit attached to that image or obtain permission.** This is why `B30_081`, despite having an exact live publication and a clear photographer credit, remains a permission case: its specific page provides no open licence for Figure SS-1. citeturn15search1
