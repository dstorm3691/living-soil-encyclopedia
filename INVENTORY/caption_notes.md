## Caption must correct
- LSE_E11: cilantro and dill typically flower about March to May in North Texas, not January to March.
- UCI_016: 'deep roots bring nutrients up' is site-dependent, as LSE_B25 itself warns.
- LSE_B25: the plant labeled false indigo is drawn as Baptisia (herbaceous perennial), not a sub-canopy shrub.
- LSE_B17: letting water sit 24 hours removes chlorine but not chloramine; DFW water is chloraminated, so use carbon filtration or a dechlorinator rated for chloramine.
- LSE_B24: trap-crop evidence for mustard is mainly diamondback moth, not cabbage looper.
- LSE_C06: brew time 12 to 24 hours conflicts with 24 to 36 hours in LSE_B09, LSE_A06 and LSE_A10; caption should state the book's standard.
- UCI_004: non-exchangeable (fixed) nutrients are slowly available, not unavailable.
- LSE_C20: implies oats reliably winter-kill; in mild North Texas winters oats often survive.
- UCI_024: rainwater pH shown as ~6.5; unpolluted rain is nearer 5.6, roof runoff varies. Bottom line calls it pH-neutral, which contradicts the panel.
